HOW TO PLAY: Tap grass to dig. Numbers show how many creepers touch that block. Mark creepers with a 🐱 cat (cats scare creepers!): long-press, right-click, or switch the mode button to CAT. Tap a number whose cats are all placed to dig its neighbours. Your first dig is always safe.
⏱ TICKING mode: the creepers are getting impatient. Clear the field before the fuse runs out. Each safe dig adds a second.
