How every casino game's odds are set

GameHow the payout is set (RTP = 97.5%)
🎲 DiceRoll 0.00 to 99.99. Payout = 97.5 ÷ win chance. 49.5% pays 1.97×.
🪙 Coin Flip50% chance, pays 2 × 0.975 = 1.95×.

🃏 Hi-LoAfter a streak the payout is 0.975 ÷ (chance of every guess being right).
🎡 Wheel40 equal slices whose multipliers add up to 39, so the average is 39 ÷ 40 = 97.5%.
🔢 Keno10 of 40 numbers drawn. The paytable for each number of picks is scaled so the average payout is exactly 97.5%.
📍 Plinko DropThe ball goes left or right at each peg (50/50). Bucket payouts are scaled so the average is exactly 97.5%.
🔴 Color Roulette25 slots: 12 red, 12 black, 1 green. Payout = 0.975 ÷ chance: 2.03× and 24.38×.

Results come from your browser's cryptographic random generator (crypto.getRandomValues). Coins are whole numbers, so a fractional payout is rounded up or down at random in proportion to the fraction (2.4 pays 3 with 40% chance), which keeps the average exact. The games in the Luck category (Slots, Blackjack and others) use their own per-run chips, not your wallet.
