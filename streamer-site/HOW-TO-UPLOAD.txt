DANNY'S HANGOUT WEBSITE — HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE
==============================================

1. SET YOUR SECRET KEY AND PASSWORD (important!)
   Open config.php in Notepad and change these two lines:

       const ADMIN_SECRET   = 'change-this-secret';
       const ADMIN_PASSWORD = 'change-me-please';

   ADMIN_SECRET: a long random mix of letters and numbers, at least 12
   characters (just mash your keyboard, e.g. 'k8Rq2xLm9vTz4hWp').
   ADMIN_PASSWORD: a normal password, at least 8 characters.
   Don't share either of them with anyone.

2. YOUR SECRET ADMIN LINK
   Your admin panel is ONLY reachable through this link:

       yoursite.com/admin.php?k=YOUR-ADMIN_SECRET

   Bookmark it. Without the key, admin.php shows a normal "page not
   found", so nobody can even tell the admin panel exists. After the link
   you still need the password. "Log out" closes it again, then you need
   the secret link again. 10 wrong passwords = locked out for an hour.

3. UPLOAD WITH FILEZILLA
   - Connect to your host.
   - On the right side, open your website folder (often called
     public_html, htdocs or www).
   - Drag EVERYTHING inside this folder (not the folder itself) into it.
     Turn on hidden files so the .htaccess files go too
     (FileZilla: Server > Force showing hidden files).
   - UPDATING an older version? Upload everything EXCEPT the data and
     uploads folders, so you keep your clips, votes and settings.
     (config.php has 2 new settings, so do step 1 again.)

4. FOLDER PERMISSIONS
   These folders must be writable:  data  uploads  uploads/clips  uploads/builds
   In FileZilla: right-click each one > File permissions > 755
   (if uploading clips then fails, try 775).

5. TEST IT
   - yoursite.com                       -> home page with the pixel world
   - yoursite.com/admin.php             -> should say "page not found"
   - yoursite.com/admin.php?k=YOURKEY   -> login screen
   - Upload a test clip on spinner.html, approve it in admin.

HANDY LINKS TO SAY ON STREAM / PUT IN BIO
   yoursite.com/discord      -> goes straight to the Discord invite
   yoursite.com/tiktok       -> goes to the TikTok
   yoursite.com/spinner.html -> the clip wheel

WHAT TO DO IN ADMIN
   - Live status: flip ON when going live -> red LIVE banner on every page
   - Clips: approve or delete uploaded clips before they hit the wheel
   - Polls: make "what should I do next stream?" polls
   - Leaderboard: one line per person, "name | score | note"
   - Top donators: one line per person, "name | amount | note".
     #1 gets the crown on the home page. Check TikTok LIVE top gifters
     after each stream and update it.
   - Clip wall: paste his best TikTok video links for the home page
   - Builds: upload SMP build screenshots
   - Settings: next event + date (countdown), SMP IP, seed of the week,
     his Minecraft username (skin viewer), schedule text, and the
     VAULT SECRET WORD (see below).

==============================================
 🤫 SPOILERS: ALL THE SECRETS (for you and him only)
==============================================
Visitors earn XP and achievements (green XP bar at the bottom of every
page, click it to see them). Progress is saved in their browser.

THE VAULT: after 5 achievements a hidden page opens: yoursite.com/vault.html
It shows the "Vault secret word" you set in admin. Tell people in Discord:
"type the secret word in #vault to get the Vault Hunter role". Change the
word now and then. (Make a #vault channel and a role for it in Discord.)
Note: the word isn't truly uncrackable, a tech-savvy person could find it,
so use it for fun roles, not real prizes.

Secret achievements and Easter eggs:
  - Mine the ground on the home page (click the blocks). 64 blocks = secret.
    Rare diamond ore in the stone = secret.
  - Hidden diamonds: sometimes a tiny diamond hides in a corner of a box
    on any page. Click it. 5 found = Diamond Hoarder.
  - Konami code on the keyboard: ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A  -> the page explodes
  - Click the logo (top left) 10 times -> also explodes
  - Type "herobrine" anywhere -> something stares at you
  - Type "diamond", "danny", "party" or "boom" for little surprises
  - Visit between midnight and 5 AM -> Night Owl (+ maybe a visitor…)
  - Clip wheel: 1 in 30 spins is a GOLDEN SPIN
  - Visit 3 days in a row -> Regular
  - Catch Danny game (Games page): score 25+
  - The home page sky follows the visitor's real time: day, sunset, night
  - Open the browser console (F12) for a hidden message

Tease these on stream ("there are secret achievements on the site, first
person to find the Vault gets a shoutout") to get people to the site
and into the Discord.

NEEDS: a host with PHP 8.0 or newer (almost every normal web host).
If clip uploads say "too big", your host limits upload size. Ask them to
raise upload_max_filesize / post_max_size, or people can paste TikTok links.

If you change the Discord invite later, also edit discord/index.html.
The custom "page not found" needs the site in the main folder of your
domain (not in a subfolder).
